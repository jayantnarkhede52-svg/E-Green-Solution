Understanding the PM Surya Ghar Scheme

The Government of India has introduced the PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana to provide massive solar subsidy in Pune and across India. This scheme aims to solarize 1 crore households, offering up to 300 units of free electricity monthly.

How to Apply for Solar Subsidy in Pune?

Applying for subsidy can be a complex process involving multiple government portals. As the best solar company in Pune, E Green Solution handles the entire application process for you:

  1. Registration on the PM Surya Ghar National Portal.
  2. Technical Feasibility approval from MSEDCL.
  3. Installation by an Empanelled Vendor (Like E Green Solution).
  4. Submission of Installation Report and Net-metering sync.
  5. Subsidy credit directly to your bank account within 30 days.
