MSEDCL Net Metering Process in Pune: Steps and Delays

Why solar net-metering gets delayed for months across Pune sub-divisions, and how certified turnkey liaisoning achieves bi-directional grid synchronization in under 21 days.

NOV 2026 8 min read DISCOM Liaison Team

The Big Problem: Why Plants Sit Idle for 60 to 90 Days

You invested ₹2.5 Lakhs to ₹15 Lakhs on a rooftop solar system in Baner, Wakad, or Bhosari. Panels are bolted to your roof, the inverter is humming, yet you cannot turn on the master MCB because you are still waiting for MSEDCL (Mahavitaran) to install the bi-directional net meter.

Every single day your plant remains un-commissioned, you lose ₹300 to ₹3,500 in unharvested solar power while continuing to pay peak MSEDCL grid tariffs. In worst cases, consumers pay loan interest on their solar system without saving a single rupee on their utility bill.

The 3 Root Causes of MSEDCL Net-Metering Delays:

  • Transformer (DTC) 70% Quota Exhaustion: In high-solar density Pune pockets like Aundh and Baner, local distribution transformers hit the MERC statutory 70% solar feed limit, leading to instant application suspension.
  • Testing Lab Queue Backlog: Bi-directional meters purchased independently frequently get trapped in 4-to-6 week queues at MSEDCL testing test benches in Rasta Peth or Bhosari.
  • Portal Document Discrepancy: Mismatched sanctioned load vs applied solar kW, unpaid arrears on the consumer bill, or outdated ownership records cause Mahadiscom portal rejections.

The Solution: E Green's 14-to-21 Day Fast-Track Protocol

At E Green Solution, we treat DISCOM liaisoning as a core engineering discipline, not an afterthought. We eliminate bureaucratic gridlock through a structured 4-stage synchronization protocol:

PHASE 01 // PRE-INSTALLATION

DTC Capacity Audit & Technical Feasibility Sanction

Before ordering a single panel, our liaison engineers check the exact spare kVA capacity on your feeding distribution transformer at your local sub-division office. If the DTC is near 70%, we secure load reservation upfront or engineer an alternate feeder sanction.

PHASE 02 // LAB VERIFIED METERS

Pre-Calibrated NABL Lab Certified Bi-Directional Meters

Instead of waiting for DISCOM stock or civilian test queues, we supply pre-approved Secure, Genus, or L&T DLMS-compliant bi-directional meters carrying active NABL laboratory test certificates and MSEDCL acceptance batch numbers.

PHASE 03 // ON-SITE INSPECTION

Joint Inspection & Safety Compliance Clearance

Our senior electrical engineer accompanies the MSEDCL Junior Engineer (JE) and Section Officer during site inspection. We provide on-the-spot earth pit resistance tests (< 2 Ohms) and inverter anti-islanding demonstrations so the test report (Work Completion Report) is signed on day one.

PHASE 04 // FINAL SYNC

Meter Sealing, Billing Credit Activation & Portal Upload

Meter installation, DISCOM lead sealing, and generation meter synchronization are executed simultaneously. We immediately trigger the National Rooftop Portal DBT subsidy release and verify your first billing cycle reflects solar unit export credits.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I increase my sanctioned electricity load during solar net meter application? Yes. If your current sanctioned load is 3 kW but you wish to install a 5 kW or 10 kW solar plant, we submit a simultaneous load enhancement petition (Form A-1) alongside the solar interconnection application.
What happens to excess solar electricity exported to MSEDCL? Excess units exported to the MSEDCL grid are banked and carried forward each month. In March (end of the financial settlement period), unutilized surplus units are credited to your bank account at the MERC notified Average Power Purchase Cost (APPC) rate.
