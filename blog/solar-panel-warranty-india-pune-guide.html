Solar Panel Warranty in India: What Pune Buyers Should Check

A practical checklist for understanding separate panel, inverter and installation warranties before you sign a rooftop solar quote.

NOV 20268 min readE Green Solution
Short answer: A rooftop solar installation usually has several separate warranties. Ask for the panel manufacturer’s product and performance terms, the inverter warranty and the installer’s workmanship terms in writing. Duration, exclusions and claim procedures depend on the equipment and contract.

When a proposal says “25-year solar warranty”, what is actually covered? A panel’s power-output promise is not automatically a 25-year guarantee for every inverter, cable, roof penetration and service visit. Before comparing prices, ask the supplier to identify each component and the organisation responsible for its support.

The four warranties to separate in a solar quote

Panel product warranty: The module manufacturer’s terms generally address specified defects in the panel itself. Read the actual model’s certificate for the covered defects, warranty period, exclusions and available remedy. The start date and whether transfer to a future owner is permitted also matter.

Panel performance warranty: This is a separate promise concerning output under the conditions described in the manufacturer’s warranty. Check the output threshold, the measurement method and the schedule over time. A performance claim may require testing; the document should explain how the claim is assessed and what remedy is available.

Inverter warranty: The inverter converts panel electricity for use by the property. Its warranty can have a different duration, registration process and service arrangement from the panels. Ask whether inspection, replacement, labour, shipping and a temporary replacement are included or excluded.

Installation workmanship: Obtain the installer’s written terms for mounting, wiring and the work performed on the roof. Clarify how workmanship relates to waterproofing, what roof condition existed before installation and who investigates a leak. The panel manufacturer’s warranty should not be assumed to cover the installer’s work.

Performance warranty is not a fixed electricity bill

A panel performance warranty is assessed using its stated testing conditions and terms. The energy shown on your inverter app is affected by sunlight, temperature, shade, dust, wiring, inverter operation and downtime. Consequently, a month with lower generation does not by itself prove that an individual module fails its warranty.

For a sensible comparison, save the original system design and expected generation assumptions, then compare monitoring data across similar weather and operating periods. If output falls, check the display for alerts and contact a qualified service provider. Our guide to low solar generation in Pune explains initial checks. Never open electrical equipment yourself.

What to ask for before signing a Pune solar quote

  1. Exact make and model: Record the proposed module, inverter and mounting equipment. Ask how substitutions will be approved.
  2. Separate warranty documents: Request current product, performance, inverter and workmanship terms, not only a duration in a brochure.
  3. Coverage dates: Ask when each period starts, whether registration is needed and who is responsible for completing it.
  4. Costs and exclusions: Check diagnosis, labour, transport, removal, reinstallation, roof repair and inspection costs, as well as the stated exclusions.
  5. Claim route: Save the installer’s and manufacturer’s current support contacts. Ask what happens if the installer is unavailable.
  6. Changes and maintenance: Find out which cleaning, monitoring and maintenance practices are required and whether third-party repairs affect cover.

For eligible residential installations, the MNRE rooftop solar guidance describes the documents and equipment requirements applicable to its scheme. Scheme requirements and a particular manufacturer’s contract are different things; check both for your project.

What about damage after a storm?

Warranty documents often distinguish manufacturing defects from damage caused by events outside normal operation. A storm, accidental impact or third-party work may be treated differently depending on the exact terms and circumstances. Ask the installer and, where relevant, your property insurer which evidence and notifications they require. Never assume that damage is covered or excluded without checking the contract. If you see broken glass, loose cables or water near electrical equipment, keep clear and contact a qualified professional rather than touching the system.

Which records should a homeowner keep?

Keep the signed quote and scope, invoice, payment records, commissioning and handover documents, panel and inverter model numbers, serial numbers, warranty certificates, registration confirmations and the equipment manuals. Save the electrical single-line diagram, roof layout and commissioning photos if provided. For an MSEDCL-connected system, retain the relevant application and connection records as well.

A simple digital folder helps: store a copy away from the inverter app and share it with the property owner or society committee. Write down the dates on which servicing occurred and keep service reports. Preserve the full original documents rather than relying on a screenshot of a promotional warranty statement.

How to prepare a warranty claim

First note the symptoms, date, error codes and affected equipment. Save relevant monitoring screenshots and photographs from a safe location. Gather your invoice, warranty certificate, serial numbers and previous service reports, then contact the installer or manufacturer through the route specified in the warranty.

Ask for a written case number, the diagnostic process, expected next step and any potential inspection or transport charges. Do not arrange unauthorised repairs before checking the terms, and never climb onto a roof or open an inverter to collect evidence. The manufacturer decides a claim under its own warranty after the required assessment.

Frequently asked questions

Does a 25-year panel warranty cover the inverter too?

Not automatically. The inverter normally has its own warranty document and terms. Verify the model, duration, labour and replacement conditions in the quote.

Does the warranty guarantee a particular monthly saving?

No fixed saving should be assumed from a panel warranty. Bill impact also depends on generation, consumption, tariffs and the applicable metering arrangement.

Who handles roof leaks after installation?

That depends on the cause and the written roof and workmanship terms. Agree on responsibilities, inspection and repair procedures before work starts.
