Key Engineering Checklist When Selecting an Inverter

Before purchasing an on-grid inverter in Pune, ensure your technical proposal checks these four boxes:

  1. Dual MPPT Capability: If your panels are mounted on two different roof slopes (e.g. South and West), a dual MPPT inverter tracks both arrays at independent voltage peaks. A single MPPT inverter will force both strings to match, losing up to 20% generation.
  2. MSEDCL Voltage Ride-Through Tuning: Grid voltage in Pune and PCMC frequently swings between 200V and 260V. Ensure your inverter supports statutory MSEDCL grid-code recalibration so it doesn't shut down during minor utility voltage dips.
  3. Built-in DC Isolator Switch: High-quality inverters feature an integrated rotary DC disconnect switch for immediate emergency cutoff without needing external clumsy knife switches.
  4. Wi-Fi IoT Smart Monitoring: Real-time smartphone apps (like ShinePhone or iSolarCloud) enable live monitoring of daily generation, export units, and automated fault alerts.

Single Phase vs. Three Phase Rules in Maharashtra

MSEDCL permits single-phase on-grid inverters up to 5kW. For systems 6kW and above, MSEDCL mandates a three-phase net meter and a three-phase string inverter to avoid destabilizing the local distribution transformer.

Inverters operate continuously under high thermal stress. If an out-of-warranty inverter trips or experiences an IGBT breakdown, buying a new replacement can cost upwards of ₹50,000. Frequently Asked Questions

How long is the standard warranty on an on-grid solar inverter?
Growatt, Sungrow, and Solis provide a standard 5-year replacement warranty, which can be extended to 10 years for a nominal fee. For microinverters like Enphase, warranties extend up to 25 years.
Can an on-grid inverter power my house during a power blackout?
No. On-grid inverters have built-in anti-islanding protection that disconnects them during blackouts. To keep power running during cuts, you need a hybrid solar system with lithium battery storage.
