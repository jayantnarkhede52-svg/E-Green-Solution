Why Solar Generation Drops in Pune & How to Fix It

Experiencing a 20% to 40% fall in daily unit yield? Learn the engineering causes behind solar degradation in Pune and how professional diagnosis restores peak output.

NOV 2026 7 min read O&M Engineering Team

The Frustration: A 5 kW Plant Generating Only 12 Units a Day

When your solar system was commissioned, your app showed 20 to 24 units generated on a crisp sunny Pune afternoon. Fast forward 12 to 18 months, and on an equally bright afternoon, your inverter struggle to cross 12 to 14 units.

A 35% generation loss translates directly into higher electricity bills from MSEDCL. Many owners blame cloudy weather or assume their solar panels have degraded permanently. In reality, over 90% of sudden output drops in Pune and PCMC are caused by 4 preventable engineering factors.

The 4 Major Culprits Behind Lost Solar Yield in Maharashtra

CULPRIT 01 // WATER DAMAGE

Hard Water Borewell Scaling & Etched Anti-Reflective Coating

Many Pune housing societies and bungalows wash solar panels using untreated borewell or tanker water with TDS exceeding 450 ppm. Under Pune’s afternoon heat, water droplets flash-evaporate, leaving mineral calcification deposits (limescale) that fuse to the glass. Normal wiping cannot remove this film, permanently reflecting 15% to 22% of incoming photon energy.

CULPRIT 02 // GRID INSTABILITY

Midday Inverter Grid Overvoltage Tripping (Error 102 / 253V+)

In industrial MIDC sectors (Chakan, Bhosari, Talegaon) and rapidly developing residential clusters (Hinjewadi, Wagholi), surrounding solar plants export maximum power between 11:30 AM and 2:30 PM. Local distribution line voltage spikes above 253V. Standard string inverters (Growatt, Sungrow, Solis) shut down automatically on grid overvoltage protection, causing zero generation during the sunniest hours of the day.

CULPRIT 03 // ELECTRICAL DEGRADATION

Potential Induced Degradation (PID) & Earth Leakage

In Pune’s monsoon and high-humidity months, stray leakage currents between the photovoltaic module frame and crystalline silicon cells cause sodium ion migration. This creates internal microscopic short circuits across solar cell p-n junctions, leading to silent 20% to 30% power losses with zero visible surface damage.

CULPRIT 04 // STRING MISMATCH

Micro-Cracking & Bypass Diode Failure

Unqualified cleaners walking directly on solar panels cause micro-cracking across solar wafers. Over months, these micro-cracks form hotspots that burn out string bypass diodes, dragging down an entire string of 12 to 18 panels to the output of its weakest single damaged cell.
