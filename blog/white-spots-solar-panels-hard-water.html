White Spots on Solar Panels: Hard Water Guide

Understand water spots, cleaning-water quality and when to ask a solar maintenance professional.

24 NOV 2026E Green Solution
Short answer: White spots after washing may be mineral deposits, but marks can have other causes. Avoid scraping and check the panel maker’s cleaning instructions.

Why are there white spots on solar panels?

If marks appeared after washing and follow the path of dried droplets, minerals left by the water are one possible cause. Dust, bird droppings, residue from an unsuitable cleaner or damage can look different but also leave visible marks. A photograph alone cannot confirm which one you have.

Hard water contains dissolved minerals. When water evaporates, some material can remain on the glass. Repeated washing with mineral-rich water may make deposits harder to manage. Whether the marks affect output depends on their extent and the system; do not assume a fixed percentage loss from their appearance.

Does every system need RO water?

No single water recipe fits every module. Ask for the cleaning instructions for your panel model and check the water used at your site. The Trina Vertex manual lists water-quality requirements for its covered modules and recommends demineralized water when available. Other models may specify different limits.

Hardness and total dissolved solids (TDS) are related but different measurements. A TDS reading alone does not identify how much calcium and magnesium are present. If a supplier proposes RO or demineralized water, ask which measurement was checked and how it relates to your panel manufacturer's requirements. Do not assume all RO water meets every specification.

What should you do when you notice marks?

  1. Observe from a safe location. Note whether marks appeared after a wash and whether they cover a small area or many panels. Do not climb onto a roof for a closer look.
  2. Check the paperwork. Find the panel model, warranty and cleaning guidance. Ask who performed the last wash and what water and products were used.
  3. Record the pattern. Photograph the marks from a safe position if possible. Check inverter history against a comparable period, allowing for weather and seasonal changes.
  4. Ask a qualified cleaner or installer. Request an inspection and a method appropriate to the panel model. Escalate cracked glass, exposed wiring or damaged equipment to a qualified professional.

Do not scrape dry deposits, use abrasive pads, strong acids or household chemicals, or spray water on damaged panels. The Canadian Solar installation manual warns that damaged modules and wet electrical equipment can present a shock hazard and that unsuitable cleaning can damage modules. Its instructions apply to the products covered by that manual; always follow your own manufacturer's documentation.

Are the marks the reason generation is low?

Maybe, but a visual mark does not measure energy loss. Compare daily generation across days with similar weather and seasons, and check whether the inverter reports a fault. New shade, soiling, a reporting problem or a grid interruption can also affect the result. Ask a technician to assess persistent changes; do not touch wiring or attempt electrical measurements yourself.

Our solar generation troubleshooting guide covers the wider checks. For an offline monitoring app, see whether an offline inverter app means generation has stopped.

Questions to ask a cleaning provider

  • What water source will you use, and how will you check it against my panel manual?
  • How will you access the roof safely and protect wiring, connectors and surrounding property?
  • What will you do if the glass or electrical equipment appears damaged?
  • What cleaning method and products are included, and will they comply with my warranty?
  • Will you document the condition before and after the visit?

For a property in Pune, ask E Green Solution about solar panel cleaning and share the module model, roof access and a description of the marks. The team can confirm the appropriate scope before a visit.

Common questions

Does rain remove white mineral spots?

Rain can remove some loose dust, but it may not remove established mineral deposits. Avoid scraping persistent marks; ask the installer to assess them.

Can I use vinegar to remove water spots?

Do not apply vinegar or another chemical unless the panel manufacturer explicitly approves it for your model. The coating and warranty may be affected.

How often should solar panels be cleaned?

There is no universal schedule. Dust, birds, rainfall, panel tilt, site access and manufacturer guidance all matter. Arrange cleaning when needed using a safe, approved method.
