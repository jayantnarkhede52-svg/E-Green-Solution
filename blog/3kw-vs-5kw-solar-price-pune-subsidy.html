1. The PM Surya Ghar Subsidy Math Explained (Why ₹78,000 is Capped)

Many Pune homeowners ask: "If a 2kW system gets ₹60,000 and a 3kW gets ₹78,000, shouldn't a 5kW system receive ₹1,30,000?"

Under the official guidelines of the PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana (2026), residential rooftop subsidies are structured as follows:

  • First 1 kW: ₹30,000
  • Second 1 kW (up to 2 kW): ₹30,000 (Total ₹60,000)
  • Third 1 kW (up to 3 kW): ₹18,000 (Total ₹78,000)
  • Above 3 kW: Zero additional subsidy. The subsidy is capped at a strict maximum of ₹78,000 per domestic consumer.

What Does This Mean for Your ROI?

Because of this policy cap, a 3kW system offers the highest subsidy-to-cost ratio (~38% to 41% of your total EPC cost is funded by the Central Government!).

However, for homeowners consuming over 500 units per month, a 5kW system delivers substantially greater total annual cash savings because it wipes out MSEDCL's ultra-expensive top tariff slabs. Read on to see the tariff breakdown.

3. MSEDCL LT-I Domestic Tariff Dynamics: Why Sizing Matters

In Maharashtra, MSEDCL bills residential consumers on a telescopic slab tariff. The more electricity you consume, the exponentially higher rate you pay for each incremental unit:

Monthly Consumption Slab Base Energy Charge (Approx.) Effective Rate (With Taxes, FAC & Duty)
0 – 100 Units ₹5.88 / unit ~₹7.20 / unit
101 – 300 Units ₹11.26 / unit ~₹13.80 / unit
301 – 500 Units ₹15.72 / unit ~₹19.20 / unit
Above 500 Units ₹17.80 / unit ~₹21.50+ / unit

How Net Metering Multiplies Your Savings

When your rooftop solar generates electricity, MSEDCL's bi-directional net meter subtracts units from your highest tariff tier first.

If your household uses 650 units a month, your top 150 units were being billed at ₹21.50/unit, and the preceding 200 units at ₹19.20/unit. A 5kW system wiping out those top slabs eliminates over ₹6,500 of monthly bill charges instantly, achieving an astonishing payback in under 3 years!

4. Decision Guide: Should You Choose 3kW or 5kW?

Choose a 3kW Solar System If:

  • Your Monthly Light Bill: Consistently ranges between ₹2,500 and ₹4,000.
  • Appliance Profile: 1 or 2 inverter air conditioners used mainly at night, 1 refrigerator, washing machine, TV, fans, and LED lighting.
  • Terrace Space: You have a compact terrace of 250 to 350 sq.ft, or you want to leave ample open terrace space for gardening or walking.
  • Connection Type: You have a standard Single-Phase meter with 3kW to 4kW sanctioned load and do not wish to upgrade to 3-phase.
  • Budget Goal: You want the absolute maximum subsidy efficiency, spending only ~₹1.15 to ₹1.25 Lakhs out of pocket.

Choose a 5kW Solar System If:

  • Your Monthly Light Bill: Frequently crosses ₹4,500 to ₹8,500 (especially during hot Pune summers).
  • Appliance Profile: 2 to 4 air conditioners (with daytime usage in home offices), induction cooking, water pumps, microwave, and dishwasher.
  • Electric Vehicle (EV): You own or plan to purchase a 4-wheeler EV (Tata Nexon EV, MG ZS EV, Mahindra XUV400) or an electric scooter (Ather, Ola). Charging an EV requires an extra 150 to 250 units per month.
  • Terrace Space: You have an open terrace slab or bungalow roof with 450 to 550+ sq.ft of unshaded area.
  • Grid Connection: You have a 3-Phase MSEDCL connection or are willing to apply for a sanction load extension to 5kW.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Why is the subsidy ₹78,000 for both 3kW and 5kW systems?

Under the Ministry of New and Renewable Energy (MNRE) PM Surya Ghar guidelines, residential subsidies are capped at 3kW (₹78,000). While you can legally install higher capacities (e.g. 4kW, 5kW, or 10kW) to offset larger electricity bills, the government financial incentive reaches its maximum threshold at ₹78,000 per consumer number.

Can I install a 5kW solar system on a single-phase connection in Pune?

In most MSEDCL distribution divisions, single-phase net metering is capped at 3kW or 4kW to prevent phase unbalance on local distribution transformers. For a 5kW system, MSEDCL mandates a 3-phase meter. E Green Solution handles the complete load sanction enhancement and 3-phase conversion process on your behalf.

What happens if my solar system generates more electricity than I use?

Under MSEDCL Net Metering rules, surplus units generated during daytime are automatically exported to the grid. Your MSEDCL monthly bill will reflect a credit balance (banked units). These banked units are carried forward month-over-month to offset higher consumption during monsoon or festive months.

How long does MSEDCL net meter approval take in Pune?

From the date of online registration on the National PM Surya Ghar / Mahadiscom portal, technical feasibility approval typically takes 7 to 10 days. After physical installation and work completion submission, meter testing and bi-directional meter synchronization is completed within 15 to 21 working days.

Can I start with 3kW today and upgrade to 5kW later?

Yes, but only if planned properly. If you foresee upgrading, we recommend installing a 5kW-rated inverter and dual-MPPT wiring upfront. If you install a standard 3kW inverter today, expanding later will require replacing the inverter, upgrading electrical cabling, and re-applying for MSEDCL load sanction.
