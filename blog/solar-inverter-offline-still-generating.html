Solar Inverter Offline: Is It Still Generating?

Check the inverter display, router and monitoring app before assuming your panels have stopped generating.

24 NOV 2026E Green Solution
Short answer: The app says offline. Your system may still be producing electricity; check the inverter display before assuming generation has stopped.

Does an offline app mean the panels have stopped?

Not necessarily. A solar monitoring app receives data through a communication device and an internet connection. If that reporting link fails, a grid-connected inverter may continue operating normally while the app shows old readings or an offline status. An offline message alone does not prove that power is being produced, either.

Check the inverter's own display or approved local monitoring interface during daylight, if it is safely accessible. Look for its operating status, current output and any alert. Different brands use different indicators, so check the manual for your exact model rather than guessing from the colour of a light.

A five-minute check before calling

  1. Check the time and conditions. At night the panels do not generate solar power; an inactive display or offline status can be normal for some models. Check again in daylight.
  2. Read the inverter display. Note the operating status, power reading and any message. Do not open the enclosure or touch wiring.
  3. Check the app timestamp. A reading from yesterday is not live data. Compare it with a current local display when available.
  4. Check your internet. See whether another device can reach the internet and whether the router has been changed or its network name or password updated.
  5. Record what you see. Save the inverter brand, model, exact alert, app status and time. A photograph of the display can help your installer diagnose the issue.

What the readings can tell you

What you seeWhat it may meanNext step
App offline; inverter shows current outputA reporting or network issue is possible.Check router and the manufacturer's monitoring instructions.
App offline; inverter shows an alert or no output in good daylightA system or grid issue may need investigation.Record the message and contact the installer.
App offline at night; no other warningSome equipment sleeps or stops reporting after sunset.Check again during daylight, following the model manual.

These are clues, not a remote diagnosis. Cloud cover, shading, grid outages and equipment differences can all affect a reading. A net meter's export figure is not the same as total solar generation: electricity used inside the property may never be exported. Read our guide to import, export and generation for the difference.

If the router or password changed

Your monitoring device may need to be connected to the new network. Use the instructions for the exact inverter and logger; the steps and supported Wi-Fi bands vary by model. For example, Solis provides a configuration guide for specific dataloggers. Have the installer help if you are unsure.

Do not factory-reset the inverter, change grid protection settings, unplug electrical connections or assume that every device uses the same password or network band. Those actions can disrupt monitoring or affect the commissioned system. Some loggers can upload stored data after reconnection, but storage and backfill depend on the device; Solis describes the limits for its supported loggers.

When to contact a technician

Contact your installer if the inverter shows a fault, remains inactive in suitable daylight, or its local output does not recover after a grid interruption. If you notice smoke, a burning smell, damaged equipment or exposed wiring, keep away and contact the installer or an appropriately qualified electrician immediately. Do not touch or investigate the equipment yourself.

Common questions

Will changing my Wi-Fi password stop solar generation?

It may interrupt the monitoring connection if the device uses that network. It does not by itself tell you whether the inverter is generating. Check the inverter's status.

Is a Wi-Fi outage the same as a power cut?

No. A Wi-Fi outage affects communication. A grid outage can stop a conventional grid-tied system unless a suitable backup arrangement is installed. See what happens during a power cut.

Should I restart the inverter?

Do not restart or reset equipment merely because the app is offline. Check the model's instructions and ask your installer before operating unfamiliar switches or settings.
