Mono PERC vs TopCon Solar Panels in Pune: 2026 Technical Showdown

An objective comparison of efficiency, heat tolerance, degradation curves, and Levelized Cost of Electricity for Maharashtra rooftops.

Home / Blog / Mono PERC vs TopCon Solar Panels Pune

The rooftop solar sector is experiencing a historic technological transition. For nearly a decade, Mono PERC (Passivated Emitter and Rear Cell) was the undisputed king of solar efficiency. However, the solar industry has decisively entered the era of N-Type TOPCon (Tunnel Oxide Passivated Contact).

If you are procuring solar panels in Pune in 2026, installers will present quotes for both technologies. Understanding their differences will ensure you do not buy outdated technology that underperforms during Pune's scorching summers.

Head-to-Head Comparison Matrix

Technical Parameter Mono PERC (P-Type) TOPCon (N-Type)
Commercial Module Efficiency 20.8% – 21.4% 22.5% – 23.4%
Temperature Coefficient (Pmax) -0.35% / °C -0.30% / °C (Better Heat Resilience)
First Year Degradation ~2.0% (Subject to LID) < 1.0% (Zero Boron-Oxygen LID)
Annual Linear Degradation 0.55% / Year 0.40% / Year
Bifaciality Factor (Rear Yield) 65% – 70% 80% – 85%
Performance Warranty 25 Years (84.8% output) 30 Years (87.4% output)

Why TOPCon Excels in Pune's Climate

Laboratory efficiency numbers are measured at standard test conditions (STC) of 25°C. But on a sunny May afternoon in Pune or Chakan, dark rooftop solar panels easily reach operating temperatures of 60°C to 70°C.

Every degree Celsius above 25°C reduces solar output based on the panel's temperature coefficient:

  • At 65°C cell temperature (+40°C above test conditions):
  • Mono PERC (-0.35%/°C): Loses 40 × 0.35% = 14.0% power output.
  • TOPCon (-0.30%/°C): Loses only 40 × 0.30% = 12.0% power output.

This 2%–3% heat resilience advantage adds up to hundreds of extra kilowatt-hours generated every summer month.

Zero Light Induced Degradation (LID)

Mono PERC cells use P-Type silicon doped with Boron. When exposed to the first hours of sunlight, Boron and trace Oxygen form complexes that degrade power output by 2% to 3% immediately. TOPCon uses N-Type silicon doped with Phosphorus, which is chemically immune to Boron-Oxygen defects, ensuring full power from Day 1.

Which Technology Should You Choose in 2026?

Choose TOPCon if:

  • You have limited rooftop area and need maximum wattage (e.g. 580W–600W modules).
  • You are installing on commercial/industrial sheds in Chakan, Bhosari, or Talegaon where summer ambient temperatures remain consistently high.
  • You are choosing bifacial dual-glass modules, because TOPCon delivers a superior 80%+ rear-side bifaciality factor.

Choose Mono PERC if:

  • You are on a very strict upfront budget and your installer offers discounted inventory.
  • You have generous roof space where lower power density per square meter is not a constraint.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are TopCon panels approved for PM Surya Ghar subsidy?
Yes, provided the TopCon panels are manufactured with domestic cells (DCR) and listed under the Ministry of New and Renewable Energy (MNRE) ALMM directory.
Can I pair TopCon panels with standard string inverters?
Yes. TopCon panels have electrical current (Imp ~13-14A) and voltage ratings fully compatible with modern on-grid string inverters from Growatt, Sungrow, and Solis.
