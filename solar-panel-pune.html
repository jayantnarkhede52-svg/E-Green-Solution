What we check before quoting

We start with your recent MSEDCL bills, the usable roof area and when you use electricity. On site we check shade, access, roof condition and a route for wiring. For an elevated structure, we also review clearances and how you use the terrace.

The written quote lists system capacity, panel and inverter models, mounting, electrical work and the scope of net metering support. It should show equipment, panel performance and workmanship warranties separately, including exclusions and who handles a claim.

For household sizing, see residential rooftop solar. For office or shop load profiles, see commercial rooftop solar. Factory projects have their own industrial solar assessment.

Konark A Plus, Sopan Baug

The rooftop project serves shared society facilities. A July 2026 MSEDCL solar agreement is recorded on a bill for one 45 kW connection. The Konark project walkthrough shows the roof layout, mounting and equipment in photos and video.

Cost, net metering and subsidy

Roof access, structure height and the selected equipment affect the price. We estimate generation and potential bill savings using your usage, tariff and roof layout. Fixed charges and seasonal generation affect the final bill.

We help prepare the application and coordinate the MSEDCL net metering steps. The DISCOM reviews feasibility, inspection and commissioning.

Eligible residential households may receive up to ₹78,000 under PM Surya Ghar. Payment depends on scheme eligibility, compliant equipment, commissioning and application approval. See the household subsidy slabs or check current MSEDCL guidance. Societies can review the separate common-facility rules.
