Engineering Solar for Pune's Distinct Urban Architecture

Pune City features a unique blend of heritage residential localities—such as Kothrud, Deccan Gymkhana, Prabhat Road, Model Colony, Shivajinagar, Erandwane, and Camp—alongside modern upscale enclaves like Sopan Baug, Kalyani Nagar, and Aundh. Rooftops in these established wards present specific structural conditions: vintage concrete slabs requiring waterproof chemical-anchored fasteners, space constraints due to water tanks and staircases, and shadow obstacles from surrounding foliage.

As an established Pune-based solar EPC, E Green Solution specializes in customized structural engineering that maximizes sunshine harvesting across Pune's 300+ annual sunny days, reducing monthly MSEDCL bills by up to 90% while fully protecting the structural integrity of your roof.

MSEDCL Net Metering in Ganeshkhind & Rasta Peth Circles

In Pune City, rooftop solar grid synchronization is governed by two major MSEDCL circles: Ganeshkhind Urban Circle (covering Kothrud, Aundh, Shivajinagar, and Model Colony) and Rasta Peth Urban Circle (covering Camp, Swargate, Hadapsar, and Sopan Baug).

E Green Solution coordinates the complete regulatory workflow without demanding customer visits to DISCOM offices:

  • Technical feasibility verification against local distribution transformer (DTR) solar capacity quotas.
  • Digital application filing on the National Solar Rooftop Portal and MSEDCL renewable energy cell.
  • Procurement, calibration, and statutory lab testing of bidirectional net meters.
  • Joint on-site commissioning with MSEDCL junior engineers and release of direct bank subsidy.
