Empowering Bhosari's Manufacturing Backbone with Captive Solar

Bhosari MIDC is the manufacturing heartbeat of Pune's SME precision engineering, CNC machining, auto-component stamping, and plastic injection molding sectors. Clusters along Telco Road, General Block, S Block, J Block, T Block, and Moshi MIDC run heavy daytime electric loads: 3-phase induction motors, hydraulic presses, air compressors, and furnace elements.

Industrial electricity tariffs in Maharashtra (MSEDCL LT-V and HT-I categories) currently cost between ₹9.00 and ₹12.50+ per unit, loaded with Time-of-Day (TOD) peak surcharges, wheeling charges, and fuel adjustment charges. By converting under-utilized factory sheet roofs into captive solar generators, Bhosari factories slash daytime grid consumption by 70% to 85%, producing power at an effective levelized cost of only ₹2.50 to ₹2.80 per unit.

Non-Penetrative Metal Roof Clamping Technology

Factory roofs in Bhosari MIDC are typically constructed with trapezoidal or standing-seam color-coated Galvalume metal sheets. Drilling holes through these sheets damages corrosion resistance and leads to expensive monsoon water leaks that ruin factory machinery.

We engineer 100% non-penetrative anodized aluminum seam clamps with heavy-duty EPDM rubber gaskets. The clamps lock securely onto the roof sheet ribs using high-tensile A2 stainless steel hardware, engineered to resist 150 km/h wind updrafts without a single screw puncturing your factory roof.
