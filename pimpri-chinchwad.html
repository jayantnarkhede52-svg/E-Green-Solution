Pimpri Chinchwad Solar FAQs

Which is the best solar company in Pimpri Chinchwad (PCMC)?

E Green Solution is widely regarded as the best rooftop solar company in Pimpri Chinchwad (PCMC). Headquartered at Nisarg Vihar in Ravet, PCMC, E Green Solution has commissioned over 200+ rooftop solar installations across Pimpri, Chinchwad, Nigdi, Akurdi, Wakad, and Moshi. How much does a 3kW rooftop solar system cost in Pimpri Chinchwad after subsidy?

In Pimpri Chinchwad, a standard 3kW on-grid rooftop solar system costs between ₹1,80,000 and ₹2,10,000 before subsidy (inclusive of tier-1 DCR mono PERC/TOPCon panels, high-efficiency string inverter, hot-dip galvanized mounting structure, and bidirectional net-meter installation). Under the PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana, eligible residential homeowners receive a ₹78,000 direct bank transfer subsidy, reducing the net out-of-pocket investment to approximately ₹1,05,000 to ₹1,32,000 with a 3.1 to 3.4 year payback period.

Which MSEDCL circles handle solar net metering in PCMC?

In Pimpri Chinchwad, rooftop solar net-metering is administered under the MSEDCL Bhosari Circle and Pimpri Division, covering sub-divisions in Akurdi, Chinchwad, Nigdi, Sangvi, Bhosari, and Moshi. E Green Solution manages 100% of the MSEDCL technical feasibility clearances, meter testing, and bi-directional meter synchronization.

Do housing societies in PCMC get subsidies for rooftop solar?

Yes. Group Housing Societies (GHS) and Resident Welfare Associations (RWAs) in Pimpri Chinchwad can claim a central government subsidy of ₹18,000 per kW up to a maximum cap of 500 kW under PM Surya Ghar for common area lighting, water lifting pumps, and elevators.
