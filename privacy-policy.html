Privacy Policy & Data Security

Last updated: October 2026. This policy describes how E Green Solution collects, uses, and safeguards information obtained through egreensolution.in.

Welcome to E Green Solution. Your privacy is critically important to us. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by E Green Solution and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

If you contact us directly or fill out a lead form on our website, we may receive additional information about you such as:

  • Personal Details: Name, Email address, Phone number.
  • Property Details: City, Location, and Monthly electricity bill (for solar calculations).
  • Inquiry Details: The contents of the message and/or attachments you may send us.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect in various ways, including to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain our website.
  • Process your request for a free solar site survey or price quote.
  • Communicate with you via phone, WhatsApp, or email for project updates.
  • Send you information regarding government subsidies (central residential incentive schemes).
  • Understand and analyze how you use our website to improve user experience.

3. Log Files & Tracking

E Green Solution follows a standard procedure of using log files and tracking tools like Google Analytics (GA4) and Google Ads. These tools collect information such as:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) addresses.
  • Browser type and version.
  • Time stamp and referring pages.
  • Number of clicks and conversion events.

This information is used to analyze trends, administer the site, and track users' movement on the website for marketing optimization.

4. Third-Party Privacy Policies

E Green Solution's Privacy Policy does not apply to other advertisers or websites. We are using services like FormSubmit for form handling and Supabase for data storage. Additionally, our partner site Stay Willas has its own policies. We advise you to consult the respective Privacy Policies of these third-party services for more detailed information.

5. Data Security

We use appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect the personal information that we collect and process about you. However, please remember that no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.
