Residential Solar FAQ

What is the cost of solar panels for a 3BHK home?

A 3kW to 5kW system is typical for a 3BHK. Costs range from ₹1.8L to ₹3.5L before subsidies.

How much subsidy can I get?

Under PM Surya Ghar, you can get up to ₹78,000 subsidy for residential installations.
