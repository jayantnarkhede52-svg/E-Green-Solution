01

What exactly is Solar Energy?

Solar energy is simply the energy we get from the sun. We use Photovoltaic (PV) cells (panels) to catch sunlight and turn it directly into electricity.

Fun Fact:

The sun provides more energy to the Earth in one hour than the entire world uses in a whole year. We just need to catch it!

02

Why should you switch to Solar?

1. Financial Freedom

Eliminate or reduce your electricity bill by up to 90%. In Pune, electricity rates increase every year—Solar locks your price for 25 years.

2. Environment

A typical home solar system prevents over 100 tons of CO2 emissions during its lifetime. It's the cleanest energy on Earth.

3. Low Maintenance

Solar panels have no moving parts. They just sit there and make power. A simple cleaning twice a month is all they need.

4. Property Value

Homes with solar systems installed sell faster and at a higher price than those without.

03
