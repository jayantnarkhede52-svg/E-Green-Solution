Powering the Future of Kiwale, Ravet & Mamurdi

Kiwale, Ravet, and Mamurdi represent Pune's fastest-growing residential and educational corridor along the Mumbai-Pune Expressway and BRTS transit belt. Flanked by landmarks like the MCA International Cricket Stadium, Symbiosis University campus, and Punawale, this modern belt is home to thousands of young IT specialists, automotive engineers, and progressive families residing in newly handed-over row houses, modern villas, and gated township towers.

Because of its open western geography approaching the Sahyadri foothills, Kiwale receives superior unhindered solar irradiance averaging 5.4 kWh/m²/day with zero high-rise overshadowing. E Green Solution specializes in fast-track, high-efficiency rooftop solar installations that drop household electricity expenses by 90%, delivering clean, green self-sufficiency in just 2 weeks.

High-Efficiency Domestic Content Requirement (DCR) Solar Panels

To qualify for PM Surya Ghar subsidies, solar modules must be certified Domestic Content Requirement (DCR) compliant. We deploy premium Vikram Solar, Waaree, and Adani Solar 550Wp+ DCR Mono-PERC and TOPCon modules. These cells feature ultra-low temperature coefficients, ensuring optimal power conversion even during the hot afternoons along the Expressway belt.

Robust Hot-Dip Galvanized Mounting & Lightning Protection

Because Kiwale is situated near open plains approaching the Western Ghats, seasonal pre-monsoon storm winds can reach up to 130 km/h. We fabricate all mounting structures with certified hot-dip galvanized iron (IS 2062 Grade steel, minimum 80-micron zinc coating) anchored to terrace concrete columns using non-destructive chemical fasteners.

Every plant is equipped with dual chemical earthing pits, heavy-duty lightning arresters, and Class-II surge protection devices (SPDs) to safeguard home appliances against grid surges.

Rapid 2-Week MSEDCL Net Metering Commissioning

Net-metering for Kiwale, Ravet, and Mamurdi is processed through MSEDCL’s Talegaon / Dehu Road Rural divisions. E Green Solution coordinates the entire workflow without homeowner effort:

  • Distribution transformer solar hosting quota clearance on the MSEDCL portal.
  • National Solar Rooftop Portal digital application and approval management.
  • Bidirectional net-meter lab testing and seal verification.
  • Physical meter synchronization and release of PM Surya Ghar subsidy directly to your bank account.
