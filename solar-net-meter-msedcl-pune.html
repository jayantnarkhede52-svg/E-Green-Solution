End Solar Billing Frustration

Generating Solar Power But Still Receiving Inflated Bills?

You invested lakhs into rooftop solar expecting near-zero electricity bills. But month after month, your MSEDCL bill arrives with zero export units credited, or worse—a massive penalty bill. This is one of the most frustrating problems homeowners and housing societies face in Maharashtra.

In 9 out of 10 cases, the solar panels and inverters are generating perfectly, but the bidirectional net meter installed by MSEDCL (Mahavitaran) is malfunctioning, recording backwards, or communicating faulty optical data.

Navigating MSEDCL sub-divisions, testing laboratories, and junior engineers alone can take months. E Green Solution provides end-to-end liaisoning, diagnostic auditing, official lab testing, replacement sourcing, and credit adjustments.

Tell-Tale Signs of a Bad Net Meter

  • Zero Export Units on MSEDCL bill despite bright sunny months.
  • Blank / Flickering LCD Screen on the net meter box.
  • Unidirectional Bug: Meter records exported power as consumption!
  • Real-Time Clock (RTC) Failure: Corrupting TOD peak/off-peak solar credits.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is my MSEDCL bill high even after installing solar?
The most common cause is a faulty bidirectional meter recording exported energy as import consumption, or meter reader apps failing to capture export optical registers. When this occurs, you are billed as if you consumed that solar electricity!
How long does MSEDCL take to replace a faulty net meter?
Relying on standard government depot stock can take 2 to 6 months. With E Green Solution's authorized procurement process, we source an empanelled NABL-tested meter and coordinate MSEDCL installation in typically 10 to 18 business days.
Will MSEDCL credit back the solar units lost during the breakdown?
Yes. Under Maharashtra Electricity Regulatory Commission (MERC) regulations, once the testing lab report confirms meter defectiveness, MSEDCL must adjust billing based on post-replacement generation or average inverter data.
Which MSEDCL sub-divisions in Pune do you cover?
We actively coordinate across all sub-divisions in Pune Urban Circle (Rasta Peth, Padmavati, Kothrud, Shivajinagar, Baner, Hadapsar), PCMC Circle (Bhosari, Pimpri, Chinchwad, Nigdi), and Rural/Industrial Circles (Chakan, Talegaon, Baramati, Shirur).
