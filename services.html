For Homes & Societies

Residential Solar Systems

Your home's rooftop is a goldmine of energy waiting to be unlocked. Egreen Solution designs and installs high-performance residential solar systems tailored specifically for Indian households in Pune and Mumbai. Whether you live in a standalone bungalow, a row house, or a housing society, we have the right solution for you.

Our rooftop solar panels generate clean electricity directly from sunlight and power your appliances — air conditioners, geysers, pumps, lights — throughout the day. Any surplus electricity is automatically exported to the grid through MSEDCL's net-metering scheme, so your meter literally runs backwards and you earn credits on your bill.

  • Reduce electricity bills by up to 90%
  • 25-year performance warranty on panels
  • Eligible for MNRE subsidy up to ₹78,000
  • Zero-cost site survey & energy audit
  • Complete subsidy & net-meter paperwork handled

Commercial Solar Plants

Thermal Efficiency

Solar Water Heating (SWH)

Solar Water Heating is the most effective way to cut your household's carbon footprint. By converting sunlight directly into heat using advanced thermal collectors and Evacuated Tube Collectors (ETC), our systems provide a reliable supply of domestic and industrial hot water.

These systems can reliably convert over 90% of available sunlight into heat. By offsetting the use of electricity, gas, or heating oil, Egreen Solution's SWH systems provide significant financial savings and typically pay for themselves within a few years of operation.

  • High-efficiency ETC collectors
  • Reliable hot water supply >90% conversion
  • Residential and industrial applications
  • Robust stainless steel storage tanks
Outdoor Lighting

Solar LED Street Lights

Using solar energy provides a very efficient solution for street and area lighting. Our solar-powered outdoor lighting products are ideal for remote locations, common area lighting in societies, townships, and garden lighting. These systems eliminate the need for cabling and trenching, reducing installation costs dramatically.

Egreen Solution provides robust lighting poles fabricated using fine-grade materials. Our LED lights feature automatic dusk-to-dawn operation and require minimal maintenance, helping you reduce dependency on unreliable grid power while improving safety.

Agricultural & Industrial

Solar Water Pumps

Water pumping is a basic need for agriculture and manufacturing. In many regions, load shedding is a major problem that adversely affects productivity. Solar PV has become an attractive source of energy for pumping because it is reliable, pollution-free, and highly economical from a lifecycle cost perspective.

We design suitable systems for existing pumps according to the specific pump type and total head requirements. Whether for irrigation or process industries, our solar water pumps ensure a consistent water supply even during power outages.

  • Reliable power for irrigation
  • Suitable for manufacturing industries
  • Pollution-free and eco-friendly
  • Zero running cost after installation
