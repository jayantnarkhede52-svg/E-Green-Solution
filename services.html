Services FAQ: Solar Energy in Pune

How do I choose between residential and industrial solar?

It depends on your average monthly consumption. Residential systems are optimized for home appliances and qualify for subsidies, while industrial solar panel installations in pune focus on high-voltage machinery and tax benefits like accelerated depreciation.

Does the solar panel price in pune include installation?

Yes, at Egreen Solution, our solar panel price in pune quotes are all-inclusive. This covers panels, inverters, mounting structures, wiring, labor, and all government liaisoning for net-metering.

Can I install solar panels on a rented property in Pune?

Yes, but you will need a No Objection Certificate (NOC) from the property owner. Solar is a great long-term investment, so many owners in Pune are open to shared cost arrangements.
