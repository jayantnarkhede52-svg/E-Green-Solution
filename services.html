01 // RESIDENTIAL PHOTOVOLTAICS & HOUSING SOCIETIES

Residential Solar Panel Installations & PM Surya Ghar Subsidy

Explore Residential Page →

Q How much central subsidy can Pune homeowners receive under PM Surya Ghar, and what is the typical payback period?

Under the Central Government's PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana, individual bungalows and residential homes in Pune qualify for a direct DBT subsidy of ₹30,000 for 1 kW, ₹60,000 for 2 kW, and up to ₹78,000 for 3 kW and above. Housing societies can also claim ₹18,000 per kW for common utility meters (lifts, pumps, common lighting).

With Pune receiving ~300 sunny days annually, a typical 3 kW on-grid solar plant generates 360–420 units per month, slashing monthly MSEDCL bills by 85% to 90% and paying for itself in just 3.2 to 3.8 years.

  • Tier-1 Hardware: High-efficiency Vikram Solar and Waaree DCR mono PERC / TOPCon bifacial modules (550W – 590W).
  • Structure Integrity: 80-micron hot-dip galvanized mounting structures rated for 150 km/h wind loads.
  • Turnkey Liaisoning: Complete MSEDCL bi-directional net meter sanction, lab test bench clearance, and grid synchronization handled end-to-end.

Q How much terrace space is required, and does solar generate electricity during Pune's monsoon season?

A standard rooftop solar installation requires approximately 80 to 100 square feet of shadow-free terrace area per kW capacity (a 3 kW system requires ~250–300 sq.ft.). We design elevated super-structures that maintain complete terrace usability for walking and recreation.

During Pune's monsoon months (June through September), advanced N-Type TOPCon bifacial modules continue generating 40% to 60% of their peak output using ambient diffused sunlight. Surplus energy banked during summer months automatically offsets monsoon consumption under MSEDCL net metering rollover rules.

Commercial Rooftop Solar EPC & Corporate Tariff Displacement

Explore Commercial Page →

Q What is the ROI and payback timeline for commercial solar installations in Pune?

Commercial electricity tariffs in Pune range from ₹12 to ₹16 per unit. Rooftop solar generates electricity at a levelized cost under ₹2.50/unit, allowing commercial facilities to achieve full capital payback in just 2.8 to 3.5 years.

Commercial enterprises, private hospitals, IT complexes, and educational institutions can also claim 40% Section 32 Accelerated Depreciation in Year 1, significantly reducing company corporate tax liability.

  • Grid Synchronization: Fully compatible with LT commercial and HT (11kV / 22kV / 33kV) power connections.
  • Cloud IoT Telemetry: String-level real-time inverter yield and power factor monitoring accessible via smartphone.
  • Long-Term Yield: Review the quoted panel performance warranty and equipment coverage by model.

Q Can commercial solar systems handle heavy inductive motor loads and power factor requirements?

Yes. Our commercial systems utilize multi-MPPT smart string inverters (Sungrow, Growatt, Solis) equipped with dynamic active/reactive power compensation to prevent MSEDCL low-power-factor penalties. Zero-export controllers ensure seamless synchronization with diesel generators during grid outages.

03 // INDUSTRIAL MANUFACTURING & MIDC

Industrial Rooftop Solar EPC in MIDC Pune (Chakan, Bhosari, Talegaon)

Explore Industrial Page →

Q How do you mount solar modules on pre-engineered factory metal sheds without voiding sheet warranties?

In industrial belts like Chakan, Bhosari, Talegaon, and Ranjangaon, we utilize specialized non-penetrative German standing seam clamps. These clamps fasten securely to the raised ribs of Klip-Lok and standing seam metal profiles without drilling any holes, preserving 100% of the PEB manufacturer's waterproofing warranty.

For trapezoidal sheets, we use short-rail aluminum brackets with structural EPDM waterproofing gaskets and self-drilling stainless fasteners with bonded neoprene washers tested against 150 km/h wind uplift.

  • CEIG Statutory Approvals: Complete liaisoning with Chief Electrical Inspector to Government for HT charging permits.
  • Lightning Protection: Early Streamer Emission (ESE) lightning arrestors and dedicated chemical earthing grids (<5 ohms).
  • Proven Track Record: Turnkey plants commissioned for leading industrial clients, including Eagle Burgmann India Pvt. Ltd. (200 kW).

Q What are the demand charges and grid export rules for industrial consumers in Maharashtra?

Industrial consumers with HT connections can offset their peak Time-of-Day (TOD) tariff consumption during the day, drastically lowering both unit consumption and maximum demand charges. Excess exported energy is credited against off-peak or evening billing cycles as per MERC regulations.

04 // THERMAL SOLAR ENGINEERING

Solar Water Heating Systems (ETC & FPC) in Pune

Explore Water Heater Page →

Q How much electricity do solar water heaters save compared to conventional geysers?

Water heating accounts for 25% to 35% of domestic electricity bills. An efficient solar water heater reduces geyser electricity consumption by up to 85%. A standard 200 LPD system saves approximately 2,000 to 2,500 units of electricity each year, paying for itself within 2 years.

  • Collector Options: Evacuated Tube Collector (ETC) for rapid heat absorption and Flat Plate Collector (FPC) for high-pressure installations.
  • Food-Grade Stainless Inner Tanks: SS-304/SS-316 inner tanks with 50mm high-density PUF insulation retaining heat for 48+ hours.
  • Monsoon Backup: Automatic electric thermostat heating element providing hot water during cloudy monsoon periods.

Q How does E Green Solution protect solar water heaters from hard water scaling in Pune?

Borewell water in Pune often contains high levels of dissolved calcium and magnesium that cause tube scaling. We install sacrificial magnesium anodes that attract mineral ions and prevent calcification inside vacuum tubes, extending system lifespan beyond 15 years.

05 // REVERSE-OSMOSIS OPTICAL RESTORATION

Demineralized RO Solar Panel Cleaning Services

Explore Cleaning Page →

Q Why should solar panels never be washed with regular tap or borewell water?

Regular tap and borewell water in Pune has high Total Dissolved Solids (200–600 PPM TDS). When water evaporates under direct sun, it leaves behind white mineral crusts (calcium scale) that permanently etch anti-reflective glass coatings and cause hotspot cell damage.

E Green Solution uses mobile multi-stage reverse-osmosis demineralized water (<20 PPM TDS) paired with soft scratch-free rotary brushes, ensuring spot-free glass and zero mineral etching.

  • Yield Restoration: Restores 15% to 25% lost power output caused by soiling and industrial smog.
  • Flexible Maintenance: Bi-weekly and monthly automated washing schedules for residential societies and commercial facilities.
  • Verified Improvement: Pre- and post-cleaning generation verification verified via inverter telemetry logs.

Q How often should solar panels be cleaned in Pune to maintain optimal generation?

In urban residential areas (Baner, Wakad, Kothrud), cleaning every 15 to 21 days is optimal. In high-dust industrial zones (Chakan, Bhosari MIDC), washing every 10 to 14 days is recommended to prevent soiling losses exceeding 20%.

06 // PREVENTIVE ASSET MANAGEMENT

Comprehensive Solar AMC Maintenance Contracts

Explore AMC Page →

Q What is included in a Solar Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC) in Pune?

Our comprehensive AMC plans protect your solar investment with scheduled preventive health audits and guaranteed uptime SLAs:

  • FLIR Thermal Imaging Scans: Quarterly radiometric infrared scans to identify invisible micro-cracks, bypass diode failures, and hot spots.
  • Earthing & Surge Audit: Verified pit resistance certification under 5 ohms compliant with IS 3043 safety standards.
  • Electrical Insulation Testing: DC string meggering to detect cable insulation degradation and prevent Riso tripping.
  • Guaranteed Fast Turnaround: 4-hour breakdown response with direct engineer dispatch and component replacement.

Q Why do residential housing societies and factories need a professional solar AMC?

Solar plants endure extreme weather, vibration, and thermal expansion. Minor unaddressed issues like loose MC4 crimps, shaded bypass diodes, or oxidized terminal lugs can cause catastrophic fire hazards or string failures. A professional AMC maintains plant uptime at >98.5% year-round.

07 // POWER ELECTRONICS & DIAGNOSTICS

Solar Inverter Service & Component-Level Repair

Explore Inverter Page →

Q What are the most frequent solar inverter error codes, and how are they fixed?

Common inverter error codes include PV Isolation Low (Riso Fault), Grid Voltage Out of Range, Bus Voltage High, and Relay Open Error. Our engineers perform systematic DC insulation meggering to isolate damaged DC cable runs, replace blown internal surge arrestors, and adjust anti-islanding parameters.

  • Multi-Brand Expertise: Certified on-site service for Sungrow, Growatt, Solis, Polycab, Fronius, Delta, and GoodWe.
  • Component-Level Lab Repairs: Electronic laboratory replacement of IGBT power transistors, control boards, gate drivers, and DC filter capacitors.
  • Firmware & Grid Code Calibration: Updating inverter firmware to MSEDCL grid codes for seamless export.

Q Is component repair cheaper than replacing a tripped solar inverter?

Yes. A brand-new commercial string inverter can cost ₹80,000 to ₹3,50,000. In over 85% of cases, component-level motherboard or IGBT replacement costs only 15% to 25% of replacement capex and restores the unit to factory performance within 48 to 72 hours.

08 // DISCOM REGULATORY CLEARANCE

MSEDCL Net Metering Liaisoning & Discom Approvals in Pune

Explore Net Meter Page →

Q What is the step-by-step process and timeline for MSEDCL solar net metering in Pune?

Net metering approvals in Pune and PCMC take approximately 15 to 25 working days through our dedicated liaisoning team:

  • Step 1: Online registration and grid feasibility study clearance with MSEDCL circles (Rasta Peth, Ganeshkhind, Bhosari, Kothrud).
  • Step 2: Sanction letter issuance and load enhancement if required.
  • Step 3: Procurement of pre-tested Class 0.5s/1.0 accuracy bi-directional net meter and laboratory test bench clearance.
  • Step 4: Field inspection, joint meter installation, seal verification, and final grid synchronization certificate.

Q How do I resolve uncredited solar export units or incorrect TOD tariff slabs on my bill?

DISCOM software billing errors frequently result in omitted export units or incorrect commercial tariff assignments. We audit your inverter export telemetry against MSEDCL monthly bills, represent your case at the division office, and secure retroactive unit adjustments and billing credit refunds.

09 // GENERATION UPGRADES & REPOWERING

Solar Plant Revamping & Repowering in Pune

Explore Repowering Page →

Q How does solar repowering double electricity output on an existing terrace?

Older solar plants installed before 2019 utilize obsolete 250W–330W polycrystalline panels with low (14%–16%) cell efficiency that suffer from PID degradation and yellowed backsheets. By replacing them with modern 580W+ N-Type TOPCon bifacial modules (22.8% efficiency), you generate 2× to 3× more clean electricity on the exact same terrace space without buying additional roof area.

  • Structure Reutilization: Structural load re-calculation allows property owners to reuse existing mounting columns, saving up to 45% in capex.
  • Inverter Upgrades: Modern multi-MPPT high-efficiency string inverters that eliminate string mismatch losses.
  • Tax Advantage: Full 40% Section 32 accelerated depreciation applies to newly installed repowering hardware.

Q When is the right time to repower an aging rooftop solar plant?

If your solar plant's annual generation has declined by >20%, or if frequent inverter tripping and burnt junction boxes require constant repairs, repowering typically pays for itself within 2.5 to 3 years through doubled electricity harvest.

Q What should property owners do immediately if a solar plant trips or smells burnt?

Immediately switch off the AC isolator switch next to your inverter, then rotate the DC isolator switch to OFF. Do not touch bare cabling or attempt to disconnect MC4 connectors under load. Contact our emergency dispatch line immediately for safe fault isolation.
