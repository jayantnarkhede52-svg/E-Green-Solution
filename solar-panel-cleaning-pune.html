The Cost of Soiling

Why Dirty Solar Panels Cost You Thousands in Electricity Bills

Across Pune, Pimpri Chinchwad, and industrial belts like Chakan and Bhosari, dry road dust, industrial exhaust particulate, pollen, and bird droppings deposit a sticky film over photovoltaic glass. This is known in solar engineering as soiling loss.

Even a light coating of dust decreases sunlight absorption by 10% to 15%. In heavy dust corridors near highway construction or manufacturing hubs, generation drops by as much as 25% to 30%. For a 10kW residential system, this represents a recurring loss of ₹2,500 to ₹4,000 every single month.

Scientific Engineering Approach

Our 4-Stage Solar Washing Methodology

We do not use abrasive wipers or hard borewell water that degrade your anti-reflective coating. Our standard operating procedure employs low-TDS demineralized water, ultra-soft non-abrasive solar brushes, and early-morning washing schedules to prevent thermal shock.

1. Pure Demineralized Water

We bring specialized reverse osmosis (RO) demineralized water with TDS below 20 PPM. This prevents calcification, mineral staining, and stubborn white salt deposits.

2. Soft Microfiber & Nylon Brushes

Zero metal scrapers. We deploy dual-action rotating soft bristle brushes mounted on telescopic carbon-fiber poles that dislodge bird droppings without micro-scratching.

3. Thermal Shock Safety Window

Cleaning is strictly scheduled between 6:00 AM – 8:30 AM or after 5:30 PM. Spraying cold water on 60°C midday solar glass induces thermal fracture and voids manufacturer warranties.

4. Inverter Yield Verification

Before leaving your premises, our technicians cross-reference real-time generation on your solar inverter app (Growatt, Sungrow, Solis) to verify active wattage improvement.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is regular solar panel cleaning essential in Pune?
Pune experiences significant airborne dust and vehicle emissions, particularly in Chakan, Bhosari, and Hinjewadi. Dust accumulation (soiling) blocks sunlight from reaching solar cells, reducing power yield by 15% to 25%. Regular cleaning ensures you generate the full units you invested for.
Can I use regular tap water or detergent to clean my solar panels?
No. Tap and borewell water in Pune contain high dissolved solids (TDS) and minerals that cause stubborn white scale deposits (calcification) upon drying. Detergents leave film residues that attract even more dust. We exclusively use demineralized RO water (< 20 PPM TDS).
How often should solar panels be cleaned in Pune?
For residential rooftop systems in Pune, every 15 to 20 days is ideal. For industrial plants situated along MIDC corridors near manufacturing and heavy transport routes, bi-weekly cleanings are strongly advised.
What time of day is solar cleaning carried out?
Our technicians wash panels exclusively in the early morning (before 8:30 AM) or late afternoon (after 5:30 PM). Washing panels in hot afternoon conditions can cause thermal shock and crack the solar glass due to extreme temperature differences.
