Leading the Solar Revolution in Pimpri Chinchwad (PCMC)

Pimpri Chinchwad (PCMC) is one of India's fastest-growing urban smart cities. With wide terraces and high solar irradiance, residents across Pimpri, Chinchwad, Akurdi, Nigdi, Sambhajinagar, and Thergaon are adopting rooftop solar to counteract rising domestic power tariffs.
