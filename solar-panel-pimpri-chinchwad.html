Precision Structural Framing & Smart Inverter Engineering

Every rooftop installation in PCMC uses hot-dip galvanized mounting structures fabricated to IS 2062 standards, with pre-drilled structural members hot-dip galvanized with a minimum 80-micron zinc layer to ensure 25+ years of zero-rust durability. We pair these structures with smart string inverters featuring dual Maximum Power Point Trackers (MPPT) and built-in Wi-Fi diagnostics, giving you minute-by-minute generation data directly on your smartphone.
