# E Green Solution — SEO audit

**Audit date:** 24 November 2026  
**Site:** https://www.egreensolution.in/  
**Search Console property:** sc-domain:egreensolution.in  
**Search data:** 23 August–21 November 2026, web search. URL inspection reflects Google's last recorded crawl, not necessarily the live response today.

## Executive assessment

The site is crawlable and its basic on-page implementation is generally sound. Its larger SEO challenges are low visibility for commercially relevant Pune searches, incomplete migration of Google's selected canonical URLs from the apex domain to www, and the lack of an indexed services hub. Prioritize these and credible local evidence over cosmetic metadata changes.

Search Console reports **61 clicks, 3,049 impressions, 2.00% CTR, and average position 26.7** over 91 days. Average position blends unrelated queries, locations and devices and is not a ranking for a single keyword. The available data is small and query reports omit some searches; do not add query rows to estimate total traffic.

## Prioritized findings

### 1. Monitor canonical migration after a confirmed redirect fix — high

- The live apex homepage and `/solar-panel-pune` now each return a permanent **308** to their www equivalent. This improves on the 307 observed in the 12 November audit. The homepage inspection now selects www, and the apex homepage is reported as a redirect.
- Google last inspected `www/solar-panel-pune` on 20 November and still selected the apex version as canonical. Inspections of the PCMC page, housing society article, net-metering page and commercial page also selected apex, but their recorded crawls were in October. These are historical index records, not proof that the present 308 is broken.
- Keep redirects, sitemap entries, canonical tags and internal links aligned with www. Reinspect priority pages after Google recrawls and monitor both host variants. Do not revert the redirect solely because the transition is incomplete. Google's documentation describes permanent redirects as a canonical signal: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/301-redirects
- `http://egreensolution.in/about.html` takes three permanent hops before the final URL. Simplifying the chain where the host permits is worthwhile but lower priority than retaining a reliable permanent redirect; verify before changing infrastructure.

### 2. Services page discovered but not indexed — high

Search Console classifies https://www.egreensolution.in/services as **Discovered – currently not indexed**. The live page returns 200, has a self-canonical, a descriptive title and one H1, and is linked from the homepage and site navigation. The tool's 46-page crawl observed 45 incoming links, mainly sitewide links; more links alone may not solve this. Review the hub for distinct value, clear service descriptions and links to detailed pages, then use URL Inspection to request a recrawl if appropriate and monitor the outcome. The reason Google has not indexed it is not established.

The live `/blog/` redirects to `/blog`, which Search Console reports indexed. Do not mistake the unindexed slash variant for an unindexed blog.

Examples from the 91-day query report:

Improve the Pune installation, residential and industrial pages using verifiable project-specific facts: capacity, location, roof constraints, installation scope, photographs, commissioning date, applicable warranties and actual results where permission and records exist. Explain the survey, quotation, installation and net-metering process and give clear next steps. Do not fabricate testimonials, project outcomes or qualifications. Link to relevant project examples within the body copy. Assess results over several weeks rather than assuming a change caused movement.

### 4. Build on existing content that already earns attention — high

The housing-society article received **13 clicks from 436 impressions**, average position **10.0**, in the period, under the apex URL. Its current body already links to the Konark case study and Pune installation page; retain those links. Review its accuracy, add verified practical details on society decision-making and common-area loads, and make the route to a relevant consultation clear. The article has a different informational intent from the project page; overlapping visibility alone does not establish harmful cannibalization.

The PCMC page recorded **213 impressions / 1 click** under apex and another **8 / 0** under www; Baner/Bavdhan recorded **114 / 1** and **47 / 0** respectively. These are page-level aggregates with differing query mixes, not a controlled CTR test. Inspect page-query combinations in Search Console, assess search intent and snippet accuracy, and improve specific titles or descriptions only where the evidence supports it. Do not add unsupported claims or keyword-stuff.

The MSEDCL bill article recorded **43 impressions and 2 clicks** on www. The net-metering service page had **61 impressions and no clicks** on apex. Check whether a contextual link between these pages is useful to the reader and ensure the service page clearly explains the actual process, scope and limitations.

### 5. Local credibility and measurement — high

Verify the current business name, address, phone number, hours and service area against the Google Business Profile and major listings before changing them. The earlier audit identified differing addresses in a directory and the website; the correct address is not established by this audit. Use real installation photos, documented credentials and genuine reviews, and respond to customers through the business's normal process. Google Business Profile and GA4 were not connected to this audit, so map-pack rankings, profile completeness and organic lead conversion were not verified.

Set up or verify conversion tracking for successful form submissions, phone taps and WhatsApp contact, with consent and deduplication appropriate to the site. Measure actual qualified leads separately from clicks; do not label every tap a completed lead.

### 6. Check the local Journey page before deployment — medium

The modified local homepage links to `/journey`, and `journey.html` is currently untracked in the local checkout. The live URL returns **404** and is unknown to Google. This is a local-versus-production difference, not evidence that the current live homepage contains a broken link. If the new homepage is deployed, include and test the Journey page and its assets in the same release. Add it to the sitemap only if it is intended as an indexable, useful search landing page.

### 7. Validate incentives and calculator assumptions — medium

The local homepage already qualifies subsidy and savings estimates as illustrative and subject to eligibility and approval. Keep that qualification, review all incentive amounts and eligibility rules against current official scheme and MSEDCL guidance, and date substantive updates. Verify that calculator assumptions and outputs accurately reflect the supported customer and system types. Avoid guaranteeing government approval or disbursement without a real contractual basis. The old `/solar-subsidy` URL currently redirects permanently to `/residential-solar`; make sure the destination answers the old page's core intent.

### 8. Performance remains unmeasured — medium

The free PageSpeed Insights API returned HTTP 429 due to shared anonymous quota. No current mobile Lighthouse score, LCP, INP, CLS or field Core Web Vitals is established. Run PageSpeed Insights manually or with a free API key for the homepage, Pune page, residential page and a representative blog page; record mobile lab metrics and any available CrUX field data separately. Then investigate actual largest contentful elements, image payloads, rendering and third-party scripts. Do not present estimates or earlier results as current measurements.

### 9. Structured data and metadata — low

The homepage's six JSON-LD blocks parsed; the limited validator passed Organization, BreadcrumbList and FAQPage, and did not assess the other three types. The sampled housing article's BreadcrumbList and BlogPosting passed the same limited check. Passing is not proof of Google eligibility. Verify business details and only add sameAs links to confirmed official profiles. Google no longer displays FAQ rich results, so FAQ schema is not a shortcut to a search enhancement: https://developers.google.com/search/updates

An independent fetch of all 45 sitemap entries found unique titles, descriptions and canonical values, one H1 per page, no JSON-LD parsing errors, and alt attributes present on all 192 image elements. This is a mechanical check: it does not establish that alt text, descriptions, structured data or page content are useful or accurate. The PCMC page lacks Open Graph tags, a social sharing improvement rather than a direct search-ranking fix.

## Crawl and indexing checks

- robots.txt returned 200 and advertises the sitemap. It allows `/`, disallows `/admin/` and `/solar-hero.html`. A robots disallow can prevent a crawler from seeing a noindex directive; verify desired treatment of private and non-indexable pages separately.
- The live sitemap is valid XML and lists **45 URLs**, all on the preferred host. All 45 returned 200 to the sitemap tool's HEAD checks. Search Console lists the sitemap with zero errors and warnings, submitted 20 November. Sitemap submission and a 200 response do not guarantee indexing.
- 41 sitemap entries lack `lastmod`. It is optional; add it only where a reliable, substantive modification date is available. Do not generate a new date on every build. See https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/sitemaps/build-sitemap
- A bounded internal-link crawl fetched **46 pages**, all at depth two or less from the homepage, and reported no broken internal links. The crawl cannot establish that pages outside its discovered set are not orphaned. Compare the complete intended URL inventory against crawl and sitemap data when reviewing additions.
- Google indexed `/blog`, residential solar, industrial solar, Baner/Bavdhan and Chakan in the sampled inspections. `/services` was discovered but not indexed. Selected canonical statuses differ among older page inspections, as described above. These examples are not a complete coverage report.

## Recommended sequence

1. Keep and verify permanent www redirects; monitor priority URL Inspection records after recrawls.
2. Review `/services` for distinct usefulness, then inspect and monitor its indexing.
3. Improve the Pune, residential and industrial commercial pages with real evidence, clear service scope and relevant project links.
4. Update the housing-society article and review PCMC, Baner and net-metering page queries; preserve useful existing internal links.
5. Reconcile real-world business details and establish reliable organic lead tracking.
6. Verify the Journey page and assets before any release containing its links. Measure Core Web Vitals with free PageSpeed Insights when quota permits.

## Scope and limits

Read-only checks included Google Search Console performance, sitemap and 16 URL inspections; public live redirects, robots, sitemap, a bounded internal-link crawl, sampled structured data, and a 45-page HTML metadata scan. No website, Search Console setting, sitemap submission, indexing request or listing was changed. Local files contain pre-existing modifications and may differ from production. Search Console data is aggregated and may omit anonymized queries. No paid tool was used; rankings, lead growth and indexing are not guaranteed.
