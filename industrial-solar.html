INDUSTRIAL ENGINEERING STANDARDS

Scalable Solar EPC for Manufacturing Plants

We design and construct high-voltage rooftop plants that harmonize with heavy inductive loads, variable frequency drives, and overhead crane vibrations.

Zero Roof Penetration

Specialized aluminum standing seam and trapezoidal sheet clamps preserving shed waterproofing warranties and eliminating rust.

CEIG Electrical Clearances

Complete end-to-end statutory documentation, safety drawing approval, and physical site inspection clearance from the Chief Electrical Inspector.

Harmonic & Power Factor Sync

Advanced filtering inverters and APFC panel coordination preventing grid penalty charges and reactive power imbalances.

ESE Lightning Protection

Early Streamer Emission lightning arrestors providing 100m+ radius umbrella protection over your factory roofs and electrical assets.

Safe Walkway & Lifelines

FRP anti-skid roof walkways and continuous stainless-steel safety lifelines for seamless, OSHA-compliant O&M cleaning access.

Cloud SCADA Monitoring

Centralized industrial SCADA telemetry displaying string currents, irradiation sensor data, and transformer temperature in real-time.
