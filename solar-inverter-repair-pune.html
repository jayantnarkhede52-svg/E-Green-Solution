The Brain of Your Solar System

Restoring Grid Synchronization & Power Generation

The solar inverter is the most complex component of your entire installation. It converts raw Direct Current (DC) from your rooftop panels into clean Alternating Current (AC) and synchronizes with MSEDCL grid frequency at 50 Hz.

Because inverters contain active power electronics (IGBT modules, bridge rectifiers, microprocessors, and high-frequency transformers), they account for over 70% of all solar operational failures in Maharashtra. High ambient heat, grid surges, and monsoon humidity often cause premature trips.

Rather than prematurely replacing an entire unit costing ₹40,000 to ₹1,50,000, E Green Solution provides advanced component-level diagnostic and repair capabilities that save you money and minimize downtime.

Error Code Diagnostics

Common Inverter Error Codes We Fix

Is your inverter display displaying one of these common fault warnings? Here is what they indicate and how we solve them.

Grid Lost / Vac Out of Range

Error Codes: 102, 302, 02, F01

MSEDCL grid voltage spikes above 253V or drops below 195V. We recalibrate AC voltage ride-through parameters and verify neutral-earth voltage on the AC distribution board.

PV Isolation Low / Riso Fault

Error Codes: 105, 301, E005, PV Isolation Low

Indicates DC current leaking to ground through cracked cables or water ingress. We trace strings with a 1000V Megger, find the damaged point, and re-terminate using IP68 waterproof glands.

Over-Temperature / Fan Stall

Error Codes: 111, 401, Err-OT, Fan Fail

Inverter thermal sensor detects over 75°C heatsink temperature, causing output derating. We replace worn brushless cooling fans and re-apply thermal compound on internal heat sinks.

Relay Check Fail / Hardware Trip

Error Codes: 201, 502, HW Fail, Relay Defect

Grid-tie relays inside the inverter have pitted contacts or burnt driver coils. We service board-level relays or coordinate factory PCB board replacement under warranty.

Should You Repair or Replace Your Solar Inverter?

We provide an honest assessment of whether a component repair or modern replacement makes economic sense for your plant.

When Repair is the Best Choice

  • Inverter is under 5 years old (Active manufacturer warranty)
  • External cooling fan failure or minor sensor drift
  • Grid code parameter mismatch after MSEDCL transformer shift
  • Blown AC/DC bridge fuses or burnt connector terminals
  • Repair cost is under 30% of replacement value

When Replacement is Recommended

  • Inverter is over 8–10 years old with low 92% efficiency
  • Multiple catastrophic IGBT silicon die explosions
  • Manufacturer discontinued parts and communication boards
  • Upgrading from single-phase to 3-phase net metering
  • New modern inverters provide 98.6% efficiency + built-in AFCI safety

Frequently Asked Questions

Which solar inverter brands do you service in Pune?
We service all leading on-grid and hybrid brands including Growatt, Sungrow, Solis, Fronius, Delta, GoodWe, SMA, Enphase microinverters, Polycab, and Havells.
My inverter shows 'Grid Lost' but power is active in my home. Why?
Solar inverters require narrow voltage (typically 195V–253V) and frequency (49.5Hz–50.5Hz) parameters to export safely to MSEDCL. While household appliances operate on wide voltage fluctuations, the inverter disengages to prevent damaging the utility grid. We recalibrate grid-tie limits within statutory rules.
Can you claim warranty if my original installer is out of business?
Yes. Solar inverter warranties are held by the manufacturer and tied to the unit's serial number. As authorized EPC contractors in Maharashtra, we can generate the diagnostic reports required by brands like Growatt and Solis to process replacement warranty units.
Do you provide loaner inverters while mine is in for repair?
For commercial clients and housing societies experiencing extended warranty replacement turnaround, we maintain backup loaner string inverters to keep your generation alive and prevent high monthly electricity bills.
