# Pune solar content research and implementation

Date: 24 November 2026. Scope: homepage service cards, residential and commercial landing pages, the housing-society content path, and project-photo attribution. Search results were sampled with free search tools; they are not a personalized or location-controlled ranking report.

## Search intent and page roles

| Audience and observed query | Best-fit page | What the visitor needs next |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Solar installation in Pune | `/solar-panel-pune` | A local survey route, phone number, what is included in a quote, net-metering support and examples of relevant work. |
| Solar panels for a Pune home | `/residential-solar` | Bill and roof checks, an understandable quote breakdown, eligible subsidy information, metering and equipment warranty terms. |
| Commercial rooftop solar in Pune | `/commercial-solar-pune` | A proposal for the actual load and roof, business operating hours, equipment and construction scope, metering, maintenance and financial assumptions. |
| Solar system for a housing society in Pune | `/blog/housing-society-solar-pune-rules-subsidy` | Common-meter bills, committee approval, roof access, a clear distinction between eligible society assistance and household assistance, and links to service and project information. |
| MSEDCL solar bill / connection | `/blog/msedcl-solar-bill-explained-pune` and `/solar-net-meter-msedcl-pune` | A clear explanation of import, export and billed charges, followed by a route to help with the connection. |

Prior Search Console data for 23 August–21 November 2026 showed 31 impressions for “solar installation in pune”, 30 for “solar panel installation in pune”, and 42 for “solar system for housing society in pune”; each reported zero clicks. These small query-level counts are directional, not keyword-volume estimates. The housing-society article had 13 clicks and 436 impressions at page level. Query and page rows are different aggregations and should not be added together.

## What the free research showed

- [Aerem's Pune installation page](https://www.aerem.co/solar-financing-installation-company-pune) combines a contact form with capacity, costs, subsidy and project-specific caveats. Its published numbers are that company's estimates; they are not E Green Solution quotes.
- [Somaya Solar's Pune EPC page](https://www.somayasolar.com/pune/solar-epc-company) addresses the roof, load profile, operating schedule, grid connection and project scope. These topics are relevant to a commercial buyer, but should be explained in E Green Solution's own words and supported by its own evidence.
- [MSEDCL's rooftop portal](https://www.mahadiscom.in/ismart/) publishes the central assistance slabs and a separate GHS/RWA framework. Its figures and rules can change; confirm eligibility and current requirements before quoting.
- [Google's people-first content guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content) favors useful original content and clear sourcing. Its [keyword guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/help/office-hours/2023/january) says there is no optimal keyword-density percentage. The separate keyword map is an editorial inventory, not a guarantee or a spam threshold.

The residential image is the Wadgaon Budruk society photograph; the commercial image is labeled Bhagawati Industries in the existing project portfolio; the industrial image is labeled Kotling Enterprises.

The user confirmed Konark A Plus is a housing society in Sopan Baug. The mismatched photographs were removed from its case-study context and their gallery captions corrected; unverified video attribution was held back on the case-study page.
