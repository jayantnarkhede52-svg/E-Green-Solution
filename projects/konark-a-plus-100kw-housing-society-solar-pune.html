1. Client Profile & Pre-Solar Electricity Bill Audit

Konark A Plus Co-operative Housing Society (Konark A Plus Saha Gruh Rachana Sanstha) is a prestigious residential community situated in Sopan Baug, Ghorpadi, Pune (Pin Code: 411001). Like most premium high-rise residential complexes in Pune, the society operates heavy common infrastructure round-the-clock:

  • Multiple high-speed residential passenger lifts operating 24x7 across all towers.
  • High-capacity underground sump to overhead tank water pumping systems and pressure booster pumps.
  • Sewage treatment plants (STP), swimming pool filtration plants, and rainwater harvesting pumps.
  • Extensive perimeter campus security, landscape illumination, and podium lighting.

The Bill Audit: Average 12,000 to 14,000 Units/Month

An exhaustive audit of the society’s official MSEDCL electricity bill (Consumer No: 160250379894, Rasta Peth Division, St. Mary Sub-Division) revealed massive recurring expenditure. Over a 13-month cycle, the society’s average monthly consumption hovered between 12,000 and 14,200 units, with peak bills exceeding ₹3,24,000 per month (and peaking up to ₹4,77,000 in heavy seasons).

Why is the MSEDCL Unit Rate Over ₹20 for Residential Societies?

Under Maharashtra Electricity Regulatory Commission (MERC) LT-I A Residential tariffs, when high-rise societies consume in excess of 500 units on a common meter, energy charges fall into the highest slab. When combined with fixed demand charges (45 kW sanction), Time-of-Day (TOD) peak surcharges, Wheeling Charges (@ ₹1.60/unit), Fuel Adjustment Charges (FAC), and 16% Maharashtra Electricity Duty, the net landed cost consistently exceeds ₹20 to ₹22.70 per unit. This massive rate makes solar adoption extraordinarily profitable!

2. Engineering Sizing & Terrace Layout Criteria

Designing a solar plant for a housing society requires precise engineering to balance available terrace space against monthly utility targets.

The 100kW Sizing Formula:

In Pune's climate conditions, a well-engineered solar PV system reliably delivers 120 kWh (units) per kilowatt per month (approx. 4 units/kW/day).

Target Monthly Generation = 12,000 Units
Specific Solar Yield in Pune = 120 Units / kW / Month
Required Solar Capacity = 12,000 ÷ 120 = 100 kWp

Shadow-Free Rooftop Area Requirement:

As specified in our engineering standards, 60 sq. ft. of shadow-free terrace area is required per 1 kW of solar installation.

  • Terrace Area for 100 kW: $100\text{ kW} \times 60\text{ sq. ft.} = \mathbf{6,000\text{ sq. ft.}}$ of shadow-free terrace space.
  • Elevated Structure Engineering: To ensure residents can continue using the rooftop for recreational walking and clothes drying, and to allow easy waterproofing maintenance, E Green Solution fabricated customized hot-dip galvanized elevated superstructures with 8 to 10 feet clearance above the roof slab.

6. Crucial Comparison: Residential Society vs Commercial Solar Net Metering

One of the most frequent questions we receive from society managing committee members and commercial property owners is how MSEDCL solar net metering regulations differ between residential and commercial connections:

Parameter Residential & Housing Societies Commercial & Office Buildings
Net Metering Banking 100% 24x7 Banking: Units generated at noon offset units consumed at night (lifts, pumps, streetlights). Daytime Instantaneous Offset: Solar offset is credited primarily against active daytime hours.
Expected Bill Reduction Up to 90% reduction on total monthly bill. ~50% reduction on daytime consumption without storage.
Central Government Subsidy ₹18,000/kW for societies; up to ₹78,000 for individual homes. No direct subsidy, but eligible for 40% accelerated tax depreciation.
Night Load Optimization Surplus banked daytime units cover all night lighting and pumping automatically. Requires Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS) for night offset (higher capex, but strong long-term ROI).
Return on Investment (ROI) 2.5 to 3 years (due to high ₹20+/unit residential tariff). 3.5 to 4 years based on commercial tariff structure.

7. Step-by-Step Roadmap: How Pune Housing Societies Can Go Solar

Implementing rooftop solar across a large residential complex involves committee coordination and statutory approvals. E Green Solution provides complete turnkey execution:

  1. Free Rooftop Feasibility Audit: Our engineers visit your society, inspect terrace shadow patterns, measure square footage (60 sq. ft./kW), and review the last 12 months of MSEDCL light bills.
  2. AGM / SGM Proposal & Presentation: We prepare a customized technical and financial dossier and present it directly to your society’s Managing Committee or General Body to address questions on terrace access, waterproofing, and ROI.
  3. MSEDCL Net Meter Sanctioning: We handle complete online MSEDCL documentation, transformer load verification, and grid interconnection feasibility approvals.
  4. Fabrication & Module Installation: Rapid erection of hot-dip galvanized elevated structures and Tier-1 Vikram Solar modules within 20 to 30 working days.
  5. Net Meter Testing & Commissioning: MSEDCL inspection, installation of the bi-directional net meter, and grid synchronization.
  6. PM Surya Ghar Subsidy Disbursal: Complete documentation assistance to ensure the society receives the ₹18,000/kW central subsidy credited directly into the society bank account.
