9-Foot Elevated Zero-Shadow Terrace Pergolas

Terrace space in Baner, Balewadi, and Bavdhan is premium real estate. Nobody wants low-lying panel racks that clutter the terrace floor and obstruct movement. We design and fabricate custom 9-foot elevated superstructures using heavy-duty hot-dip galvanized iron (80+ micron zinc bath) with diagonal wind braces.

This elevated canopy functions like an architectural gazebo: you enjoy a completely shaded sit-out space for family teas and sunset views, the upper floor stays 3°C to 5°C cooler during Pune's harsh summer months, and you generate clean green electricity overhead.

Bifacial N-Type TOPCon Modules: High Generation in Compact Footprints

To maximize generation on limited urban terrace areas, we deploy high-efficiency Tier-1 Bifacial Dual-Glass N-Type TOPCon solar modules (550Wp+ to 590Wp+). Because bifacial panels capture direct sunlight on the top face while simultaneously harvesting reflected ambient light (albedo) from the terrace floor on the underside, you gain up to 15% to 25% higher electricity yield compared to conventional solar panels.
