High-Voltage (HT) Grid Synchronization & CEIG Approvals

Connecting large industrial solar plants to 11kV, 22kV, or 33kV high-tension feeders requires rigorous statutory precision. E Green Solution provides complete turnkey engineering: structural load calculations for pre-engineered buildings (PEBs), short-circuit fault current analysis, statutory submission of drawings to the Chief Electrical Inspector to Government (CEIG), Vacuum Circuit Breaker (VCB) relay calibration, and synchronization with existing captive DG systems using smart reverse power protection controllers (zero grid export or controlled export).

Non-Penetrative Standing-Seam Clamp Technology

Preserving the structural integrity and waterproofing of expansive PEB factory sheds is our highest priority. We deploy non-penetrative, anodized aluminum standing-seam and trapezoidal seam clamps designed specifically for metal roofing profiles (Tata BlueScope, Lysaght, Kirby, and Jindal).

These clamps grip the raised seam ribs securely with zero drilling into the sheet metal. Every assembly is rated to resist 160 km/h wind updrafts and includes thermal expansion gaps to ensure zero mechanical stress on factory purlins over 25+ years of operational life.
