Project Support

Planning Each Rooftop Project

Rooftops differ in structure, shade, access and electrical requirements. We review these conditions with the customer and identify proposed modules, inverter, mounting and protection equipment in the project scope. Manufacturer and workmanship warranty terms are confirmed in writing for the selected system.

We can assist with MSEDCL net-metering paperwork and help eligible households prepare PM Surya Ghar applications. Utility and scheme authorities review and approve applications; timing and outcomes depend on their requirements.

Empowering Industries and Enhancing Lives

Our impact is felt most strongly in the industrial hubs of MIDC and beyond. By implementing large-scale EPC (Engineering, Procurement, and Construction) solar projects, we help factories and warehouses turn their idle roof space into productive assets. These systems don't just help the environment; they dramatically lower operational overheads, giving our industrial clients a competitive edge in the global market.

For residential customers, savings depend on the system design, roof generation, household use, tariff and settlement rules. For businesses and factories, a project review can compare available roof area with daytime loads and utility requirements. We document project scope and estimates so customers can evaluate the proposed system before deciding.
