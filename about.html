Our Story

The Vision: Powering Maharashtra's Solar Revolution

At Egreen Solution, we believe the future of energy is the sun. We help Pune and Mumbai switch to clean, cheap power. Our team has installed over 500 solar systems for homes and businesses. We are now one of the most trusted solar names in the region.

Our journey started over 10 years ago. We wanted to help families and businesses cut their high electricity bills. What began as a small team is now a major solar power provider. We serve everyone from small houses in Pune to large factories in Mumbai.

The Egreen Standard of Excellence

We don't settle for average. Every system designed by Egreen Solution is a testament to our "No Compromise" engineering philosophy. We strictly utilize Tier-1 solar modules and high-performance inverters from global leaders to ensure that every kilowatt-hour of energy generated is done so with maximum efficiency. Our team of certified solar engineers conducts meticulous site audits, evaluating every factor from roof structural integrity to micro-shading patterns, ensuring that we don't just install panels, but rather build an energy asset that lasts for 25 years or more.

We understand that transitioning to solar can feel like a complex journey. That’s why we’ve built our reputation on complete transparency and hand-holding through every step. From the initial feasibility study and customized system design to the often-daunting world of government subsidies and net-metering applications, Egreen Solution handles it all. We are one of the few providers in Maharashtra that offers a truly turnkey solution, allowing our clients to focus on their lives and businesses while we manage the transition to energy independence.

Empowering Industries and Enhancing Lives

Our impact is felt most strongly in the industrial hubs of MIDC and beyond. By implementing large-scale EPC (Engineering, Procurement, and Construction) solar projects, we help factories and warehouses turn their idle roof space into productive assets. These systems don't just help the environment; they dramatically lower operational overheads, giving our industrial clients a competitive edge in the global market.

Simultaneously, we remain deeply rooted in our residential mission, bringing the same level of industrial-grade precision to homes across Pune and Mumbai, helping families save up to 90% on their summer electricity bills. At Egreen Solution, our success is measured not just by the megawatts we install, but by the tons of CO2 we prevent from entering our atmosphere and the millions of rupees we save our clients. Join us in our mission to light up every roof in Maharashtra with the power of the sun. Together, we are building a legacy of clean energy that will benefit generations to come.
