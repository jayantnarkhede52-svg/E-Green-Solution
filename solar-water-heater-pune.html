How Solar Water Heaters Outperform Electric Geysers

An electric bathroom geyser consumes 2,000 to 3,000 Watts every hour. For a family of 4 to 6 members in Pune, heating water electrically accounts for 25% to 35% of total residential power consumption.

By installing a solar thermal water heater on your terrace, solar radiation heats water directly to 65°C–80°C. With high-density 50mm CFC-free polyurethane foam (PUF) insulation, water remains steaming hot overnight for morning baths, even during December-January cold snaps in Pune and Lonavala.

  • Inner Tank Protection: Food-grade SS-304/SS-316 or ceramic glass-lined enamel to resist Pune hard water scaling.
  • Monsoon Backup: Integrated 2kW/3kW thermostat electrical heating element for continuous cloudy days.
  • Zero Running Cost: Pays for itself in electricity bill savings within 2 to 3 years.

ETC vs. FPC Solar Water Heaters: Which is Right for You?

Selecting between Evacuated Tube Collectors (ETC) and Flat Plate Collectors (FPC) depends on your water hardness, pressure requirements, and roof layout.

Comparison Factor ETC (Evacuated Tube Collector) FPC (Flat Plate Collector)
Working Principle Borosilicate 3.3 double-wall glass tubes with vacuum insulation Copper riser tubes with selective coated aluminum/copper absorber plate
Best Water Type Municipal corporation water (PCMC / PMC soft-to-medium water) Hard borewell water or high mineral content areas
Winter Efficiency High (vacuum completely eliminates heat loss to cold winter winds) Moderate to Good (requires ambient warmth for peak heating)
Pressure Handling Standard gravity feed (Pressurized models available with manifold) Handles high water pressure from overhead booster pumps natively
Cost & Maintenance Cost-effective; individual damaged tubes can be replaced easily Higher initial investment; extremely rugged metal construction

Key Installation & Plumbing Requirements

  • Overhead Tank Elevation: For gravity models, the bottom of the cold water overhead storage tank must be elevated at least 5 to 6 feet above the solar water heater to ensure smooth gravitational flow.
  • South-Facing Unobstructed Sunlight: The collector tubes or plates must face True South (±15°) with zero shade from surrounding parapet walls, trees, or overhead water tanks between 9:00 AM and 4:30 PM.
  • Hot Water Plumbing Pipes: We advise using high-temperature rated, insulated CPVC pipes or composite pipes to minimize temperature drop during transit from the terrace down to bathroom faucets.
  • Air Vent Pipe: Proper vertical venting must be installed to relieve air locks and internal steam pressure during high-temperature afternoon hours.

Common Questions

Solar Water Heater FAQs

Which is better for Pune water: ETC or FPC solar water heaters?

For municipal corporation tap water (PMC or PCMC supply), Evacuated Tube Collector (ETC) systems offer the fastest heat absorption, lower upfront cost, and work remarkably well even on cloudy or winter days. For hard borewell water or high-pressure shower systems, Flat Plate Collector (FPC) with copper tubes or glass-lined ETC tanks are strongly recommended to resist mineral scaling.

How much capacity (LPD) is required for a 4-person family in Pune?

A standard thumb rule is 30 to 50 litres of hot water per person daily for bath and kitchen use. A 4-person family typically installs a 150 LPD or 200 LPD system. A 200 LPD system provides generous hot water capacity for morning showers and evening washing.

What height must the cold water overhead tank have above the solar heater?

For standard gravity-fed non-pressurized solar water heaters, the base of your cold water overhead tank should be elevated at least 5 to 7 feet above the solar water heater tank to ensure adequate gravitational feeding pressure. If you have an overhead pressure booster pump, a pressurized solar water heater model with heavy-gauge inner tank is required.

Does a solar water heater work during Pune monsoons and winter?

Yes. Pune receives strong diffuse solar radiation even during light monsoon clouds. During peak winter mornings (December-January, 11°C–14°C ambient), our high-density 50mm injected PUF insulation keeps the stored water hot at 60°C+ overnight. All our systems come with an optional thermostatically controlled electrical backup heating coil for extended rainy days.
