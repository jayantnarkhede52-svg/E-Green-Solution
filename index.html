Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to help you understand solar energy and our services.

Solar installation costs vary based on system size, roof type, and location. A typical 5kW residential system in Pune costs ₹3-4 lakhs (before subsidies). We provide free site surveys and transparent quotes. EMI options are available through banks.

Savings depend on your current consumption and system size. Most customers save 60-90% on summer bills and 40-70% annually. A 5kW system typically saves ₹50,000-80,000 yearly. Use our Solar Calculator to get exact estimates for your home.

Net metering lets you export excess solar energy to the grid and get credit on your bill. In Maharashtra, MSEDCL and BEST provide net metering benefits. We handle all documentation and approvals—you just enjoy the savings!

Typical payback period is 5-7 years with government subsidies, and 6-8 years without. After payback, you enjoy 20+ years of free electricity. We provide detailed ROI projections in your personalized quote.

Yes! Residential systems get 40% subsidy from Maharashtra govt (MAHAIPL scheme). Plus: 5% GST, capital subsidy, tax benefits under 80D. Commercial/industrial systems qualify for higher incentives. We handle all subsidy applications.

Installation takes 2-5 days for typical rooftop systems. Total approval + installation is 3-4 weeks including net metering setup. We work fast without compromising on quality or safety.

Solar panels need minimal maintenance. Annual cleaning and inverter check-ups ensure peak efficiency. We offer maintenance packages starting at ₹2,500/year, covering cleaning and monitoring.

Absolutely! We provide 24/7 support, annual maintenance, remote monitoring, and hardware warranty coverage. Our team is always available via phone, WhatsApp, or email for any concerns.
