10 // TECHNICAL KNOWLEDGE BASE

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about solar costs, system sizing, MSEDCL net metering and ongoing maintenance in Pune.

E Green Solution provides rooftop solar installation and related support in Pune. Ask for a written quote that identifies the proposed equipment, work scope and applicable manufacturer warranties.
Solar cost depends on the roof, equipment and electrical work required. Commercial and industrial systems need a site survey for an accurate estimate. See our residential incentive guide for current eligibility details.
MSEDCL net metering records electricity imported from and exported to the grid. Bill savings depend on system generation, on-site use, applicable settlement rules and fixed charges.
Installation timing depends on system size, roof conditions, electrical work and utility approvals. E Green Solution can provide a project schedule after a site survey.
Payback depends on system cost, roof generation, electricity usage and tariff. A site survey and recent bill provide a more useful estimate than a fixed range.
Maintenance needs depend on site conditions and equipment guidance. Keep modules clear of heavy soiling and arrange electrical and inverter checks with a qualified technician.
