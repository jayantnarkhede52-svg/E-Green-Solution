In Pune, a standard 3kW residential rooftop system costs approximately ₹1,95,000 before government incentives. Net metering allows your solar system to export surplus power back to the MSEDCL electricity grid through an approved bidirectional meter. At the end of every billing cycle, your exported solar units are credited against consumed units, dropping your electricity bills by 60% to 90%.

Physical on-site solar panel installations in Pune take 2 to 4 working days for typical residential rooftops. The entire turnkey timeline—including MSEDCL net metering sanction, meter installation, testing, and subsidy portal submission—is completed within 2 to 3 weeks.

Solar panels have no moving parts and require minimal maintenance. Rinsing the panels with plain water twice a month to clear ambient dust and an annual inverter and wiring check ensures peak generation over their 25-year lifetime.
