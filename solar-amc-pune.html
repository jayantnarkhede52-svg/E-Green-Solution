Why Every Rooftop & Industrial Solar Plant Needs an AMC

A solar system is a long-term power plant that operates in extreme weather conditions 365 days a year. Without routine maintenance, invisible electrical problems—such as thermal hotspots, loosened DC terminal lugs, deteriorating earthing resistance, and inverter drift—steadily compound.

Unchecked faults not only degrade generation by 10% to 20% annually but also pose critical fire hazards and grid trip penalties. Partnering with certified solar AMC contractors in Pune guarantees maximum kWh generation, extended equipment lifespan, and zero unbudgeted downtime.

Whether your system was installed by E Green Solution or an external contractor who no longer provides service, our engineering teams are equipped to take over and optimize your asset.

Technical Inspection Checklist Carried Out Every Quarter

Our NISE-certified engineers perform exhaustive 30-point diagnostics to catch microscopic failures before they cause power trips.

Inverter & Power Conditioning

Firmware check, heatsink thermal dissipation test, cooling fan operational check, DC input capacitor check, error history log retrieval.

String & DC Electricals

String-level open circuit voltage (Voc) and short-circuit current (Isc) measurement, insulation resistance (Megger test), MC4 connector tight-seal check.

Protection & Earthing

Digital Earth Resistance Meter testing of all AC, DC, and Lightning Arrestor (LA) chemical earth pits to ensure impedance stays strictly below 5 ohms.

Structure & Mechanical Integrity

Torquing of mid-clamps, end-clamps, and foundation anchor fasteners. Rust and galvanization inspection on elevated superstructures.

Frequently Asked Questions: Solar AMC in Pune

What is included in a Solar AMC contract in Pune?
Our contracts cover 4 comprehensive quarterly preventive maintenance audits, electrical health testing (inverters, strings, earthing pits < 5 ohms), thermal imaging scans, mechanical clamp torquing, and rapid response SLAs for emergency breakdowns.
Can E Green Solution take over an AMC if installed by another vendor?
Yes, absolutely. We frequently onboard housing societies and commercial plants across Pune whose original installers went out of business or failed to provide ongoing technical service. We perform a baseline site audit to ensure safe handover.
What is the difference between Comprehensive and Non-Comprehensive AMC?
Non-Comprehensive AMC covers scheduled inspections, diagnostic labor, and emergency engineering visits. Comprehensive AMC additionally covers the cost of consumable electrical components (DC/AC fuses, surge arrestors, MC4 connectors) and manages module/inverter warranty replacements without extra service fees.
How does an AMC help with MSEDCL net metering and billing?
Our AMC engineers monitor monthly generation against your MSEDCL electricity bill to detect net meter drift or uncredited generation credits immediately. If discrepancies occur, we assist with MSEDCL net meter testing and billing dispute resolution.
