MSEDCL processes net metering applications through local circle offices (Rasta Peth, Ganeshkhind, Bhosari, and Pune Rural). E Green Solution manages all paperwork: filing the initial feasibility quota, submitting technical wiring diagrams, arranging the DISCOM site inspection, installing the bidirectional net meter, and issuing the final commissioning letter.

Under the central Group Housing Society incentive programme, Group Housing Societies (GHS) and Residential Welfare Associations (RWA) in Pune are entitled to a central subsidy of ₹18,000 per kW for common utility meters (covering elevators, common lighting, and water pumping motors) up to 500 kW capacity.
