Why Rooftop Solar is Essential for Pune Commercial Enterprises

Under Maharashtra State Electricity Distribution Co. Ltd. (MSEDCL), commercial power tariffs (LT-II Non-Residential and HT Commercial) are among the highest in India, frequently exceeding ₹11 to ₹14+ per unit when including fuel adjustment charges and electricity duties. Because commercial air conditioning, illumination, computing infrastructure, and ventilation operate during peak daylight hours (9:00 AM to 6:00 PM), on-site solar generation directly displaces top-tier utility tariffs, creating an unprecedented return on investment.

Healthcare institutions require non-stop reliable power for ICUs, surgical suites, and diagnostic equipment. Commercial solar acts as the primary cost-saving energy layer, seamlessly synchronizing with dual grid lines and backup diesel generators.

Commercial Solar FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

How does commercial solar help Pune businesses reduce operating costs?

Commercial power tariffs in Pune under MSEDCL (LT-II and HT Commercial) range between ₹11 to ₹14+ per kWh. Commercial facilities run air conditioning, lighting, servers, and elevators during daytime hours (9 AM to 6 PM), which coincides exactly with peak solar irradiance. Solar power displaces expensive grid electricity unit-for-unit, typically yielding a 2.8 to 3.5 year capital payback.

Can commercial solar clients claim 40% accelerated depreciation in India?

Yes. Under Section 32 of the Income Tax Act, eligible commercial enterprises and companies investing in rooftop solar power generation systems can claim 40% accelerated depreciation in the first year of commissioning, significantly reducing income tax liability. Consult your chartered accountant or corporate tax advisor for specific entity eligibility and filing guidelines.

What commercial system sizes does E Green Solution design and install?

We engineer commercial rooftop systems ranging from 15 kW for standalone clinics, boutique offices, and restaurants, up to 500 kW+ for corporate IT parks, multi-specialty hospitals, large colleges, and shopping malls.

How does MSEDCL commercial net metering work in Pune?

MSEDCL permits grid-tied rooftop solar net metering for commercial LT and HT consumers up to their sanctioned load. A bi-directional meter records solar power sent to the grid and power consumed from MSEDCL. Surplus daytime generation credits are offset against your monthly electricity billing cycle.

Can solar panels be installed on elevated structures or carports on commercial roofs?

Yes. We engineer elevated superstructures (7–10 ft clearance) on RCC roofs that preserve 100% of usable terrace space for cafeteria seating or recreational zones. We also design solar carports over commercial parking areas, providing vehicle shade while generating clean power.
